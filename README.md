## Project Overview

The **Pragati Manufacturing Dashboard** aggregates shop-floor operational data into a centralized, interactive interface. It enables plant managers, process engineers, and executive decision-makers to track production yield, analyze machine downtime, monitor quality defects, and manage inventory flow in near real-time.

### Key Objectives
* **Increase Machine Utilization:** Identify bottlenecks and downtime drivers through Overall Equipment Effectiveness (OEE) metrics.
* **Improve Product Quality:** Monitor scrap rates, first-pass yield, and defect distribution to drive continuous process improvement.
* **Optimize Inventory & Logistics:** Maintain visibility over raw material stock levels, work-in-progress (WIP), and customer delivery timelines.

---

## Key Features & Metrics

* **Production Tracking:** Daily/weekly output analysis, target vs. actual production rates, and batch completion status.
* **Overall Equipment Effectiveness (OEE):** Breakdown of machine performance across three pillars:
  * **Availability:** Planned vs. unplanned downtime.
  * **Performance:** Standard vs. actual line speed.
  * **Quality:** Defect-free units vs. total produced.
* **Downtime & Maintenance Analysis:** Categorization of root causes (e.g., mechanical failure, operator error, material delay).
* **Quality & Scrap Rate:** Breakdown of scrap costs, defect categories, and first-pass yield trends.
* **Interactive Filtering:** Slicers for plant location, production line, shift, date range, and machine ID.

## Dataset Details

The dashboard is powered by a realistic 5,000-row manufacturing dataset designed to replicate real-world industrial plant operations. It spans four primary operational domains:

1. **Production:** Batch IDs, line numbers, units produced, target output, shift details.
2. **Quality Control:** Defect types, scrap unit counts, rework costs, pass/fail status.
3. **Maintenance:** Machine status, downtime duration (minutes), primary fault codes.
4. **Financials & Logistics:** Material costs, order fulfillment timelines, unit profit margins.
